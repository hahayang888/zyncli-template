# Monumental Metaphor style guide

## Visual DNA

Architectural 3D editorial illustration where small stylized adult workers operate one oversized matte mechanism that embodies organizational scale, leverage, or transformation.

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 horizontal
- Canvas: bright neutral studio or abstract architectural field with a strong ground plane, generous air, and one monumental object system
- Rendering language: matte sculpted 3D, clean soft bevels, realistic contact shadows, adult figures at consistent scale, and restrained structural detail
- Minimum whitespace: 24%
- Maximum subject coverage: 72%
- Typography: No labels, UI, control icons, brand marks, numbers, or pseudo-writing on the mechanism.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette 1 | `#2067C8` | primary structure and focal subject |
| Palette 2 | `#55C9A5` | supporting contrast and secondary forms |
| Palette 3 | `#F6C23E` | accent for emphasis and directional cues |
| Palette 4 | `#EFF4F6` | restrained supporting accent |

## Must

- one monumental mechanism
- two to six small adult workers
- one coordinated operational task
- clear scale contrast
- physically plausible ground contact

## Never

- toy set
- isometric icon pack
- construction advertisement
- giant laptop
- mascot characters
- random floating modules

## Negative constraints

- no text
- no logo
- no UI
- no toy plastic
- no oversized heads
- no malformed figures

## Composition patterns

### Focal metaphor

Best for: core argument, abstract concept.

Build one dominant physical metaphor around the article's central relationship.

### Contrast

Best for: before and after, tradeoff, tension.

Use two clearly different states joined by one meaningful transition.

### Process

Best for: workflow, cause and effect, transformation.

Show only the essential stages and make the change itself visually dominant.

### System relationship

Best for: framework, network, components.

Limit the system to three to five meaningful parts with an obvious hierarchy.

### Narrative scene

Best for: human consequence, decision, editorial tension.

Stage one decisive moment rather than summarizing the whole article.

## Metaphor method

1. Translate the abstract relationship into a physical arrangement.
2. Choose one object family suited to the visual medium.
3. Make the decisive change visible without relying on a headline.

Object pool:

- giant switch
- shared engine
- modular gateway
- oversized instrument
- service tower
- mechanical bridge
- large vessel
- operational wheel

Action pool:

- activate
- assemble
- calibrate
- maintain
- coordinate
- lift
- route
- transform

Anti-repetition:

- Change both the main object and the spatial arrangement between illustrations.
- Do not repeat the same composition pattern consecutively.
- Derive metaphors from the current article rather than a stock visual library.

## QA checklist

- scale is immediately clear
- workers perform a real task
- mechanism expresses the article
- materials remain matte
- scene feels editorial

Failure signals:

- looks like stock isometric art
- mechanism is generic
- workers are decorative
- toy styling dominates
- physical relationships are impossible
