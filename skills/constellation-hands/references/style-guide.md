# Constellation Hands style guide

## Visual DNA

Elegant symbolic editorial illustration using fine copper hand outlines and one concentrated constellation of amber particles to show stewardship, trust, influence, or collective intelligence.

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 horizontal
- Canvas: deep matte navy field with subtle tonal geometry, large dark breathing space, and one luminous center of action
- Rendering language: precise warm-metal linework, sparse botanical or geometric accents, controlled particle sizes, and restrained soft glow
- Minimum whitespace: 28%
- Maximum subject coverage: 68%
- Typography: No mystical glyphs, words, logos, currency signs, or pseudo-writing.

| Palette 4 | `#697484` | restrained supporting accent |

## Must

- two or three anatomically credible hands
- one purposeful particle system
- particles respond to a clear gesture
- copper-versus-navy hierarchy
- human agency remains central

## Never

- magic spell
- astrology chart
- fortune telling
- random glitter
- many floating icons
- fantasy jewelry advertisement

## Negative constraints

- no text
- no logo
- no zodiac symbols
- no religious icon
- no extra fingers
- no particle explosion without structure

## Composition patterns

### Focal metaphor

Best for: core argument, abstract concept.

Build one dominant physical metaphor around the article's central relationship.

### Contrast

Best for: before and after, tradeoff, tension.

Use two clearly different states joined by one meaningful transition.

### Process

Best for: workflow, cause and effect, transformation.

Show only the essential stages and make the change itself visually dominant.

### System relationship

Best for: framework, network, components.

Limit the system to three to five meaningful parts with an obvious hierarchy.

### Narrative scene

Best for: human consequence, decision, editorial tension.

Stage one decisive moment rather than summarizing the whole article.

## Metaphor method

1. Translate the abstract relationship into a physical arrangement.
2. Choose one object family suited to the visual medium.
3. Make the decisive change visible without relying on a headline.

Object pool:

- shared constellation
- particle seed
- luminous thread
- held network
- distributed spark
- orbit cluster
- signal dust
- common center

Action pool:

- guide
- gather
- release
- protect
- distribute
- shape
- connect
- entrust

Anti-repetition:

- Change both the main object and the spatial arrangement between illustrations.
- Do not repeat the same composition pattern consecutively.
- Derive metaphors from the current article rather than a stock visual library.

## QA checklist

- hand anatomy is clean
- gesture explains the relationship
- particle hierarchy is controlled
- glow does not overwhelm
- tone remains editorial

Failure signals:

- looks supernatural
- hands are malformed
- particles are decorative
- too many symbols
- contrast becomes flashy
