# Pastel Threshold style guide

## Visual DNA

Atmospheric chalk-pastel editorial scenes with one small adult moving between turbulent and ordered spatial fields, using powder, smudge, and light to express transition.

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 horizontal
- Canvas: toothy warm-gray paper with large powdery fields, a quiet horizon, and open breathing space
- Rendering language: broad pastel blocks, rubbed transitions, charcoal accents, selective sharp edges at the decision point, and restrained figurative detail
- Minimum whitespace: 30%
- Maximum subject coverage: 64%
- Typography: No words, handwriting, quotes, poster type, or decorative marks.

## Must

- one human threshold
- clear before-and-after atmosphere
- powdery material
- emotion through posture
- one sharp directional edge

## Never

- therapy poster
- dreamy gradient wallpaper
- childlike crayon
- fantasy landscape
- melodramatic figure
- decorative color cloud

## Negative constraints

- no text
- no logo
- no smiling success pose
- no photoreal face
- no rainbow mist
- no digital airbrush finish

## Composition patterns

### Focal metaphor

Best for: core argument, abstract concept.

Build one dominant physical metaphor around the article's central relationship.

### Contrast

Best for: before and after, tradeoff, tension.

Use two clearly different states joined by one meaningful transition.

### Process

Best for: workflow, cause and effect, transformation.

Show only the essential stages and make the change itself visually dominant.

### System relationship

Best for: framework, network, components.

Limit the system to three to five meaningful parts with an obvious hierarchy.

### Narrative scene

Best for: human consequence, decision, editorial tension.

Stage one decisive moment rather than summarizing the whole article.

## Metaphor method

1. Translate the abstract relationship into a physical arrangement.
2. Choose one object family suited to the visual medium.
3. Make the decisive change visible without relying on a headline.

Object pool:

- pastel threshold
- aligned planes
- turbulent field
- quiet chamber
- lit path
- soft barrier
- opening
- balanced horizon

Action pool:

- cross
- emerge
- steady
- leave
- approach
- reorient
- pause
- open

Anti-repetition:

- Change both the main object and the spatial arrangement between illustrations.
- Do not repeat the same composition pattern consecutively.
- Derive metaphors from the current article rather than a stock visual library.

## QA checklist

- transition is spatially clear
- posture feels adult and restrained
- pastel surface remains visible
- color supports emotion
- scene avoids wellness cliché

Failure signals:

- generic motivational art
- too soft to read
- figure feels sentimental
- digital blur replaces pastel
- no clear threshold
