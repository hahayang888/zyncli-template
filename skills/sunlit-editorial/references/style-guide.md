# Sunlit Editorial style guide

## Visual DNA

Bright concept-first editorial landscapes using broad matte color planes, simplified adult figures, layered depth, and one purposeful route through an abstracted environment.

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 horizontal
- Canvas: high-key open field or sky with broad simplified environmental planes and at least thirty percent quiet space
- Rendering language: crisp matte painted silhouettes, subtle print grain, controlled depth layers, and no glossy gradients
- Minimum whitespace: 30%
- Maximum subject coverage: 67%
- Typography: No words, labels, signs, arrows with text, or motivational headline.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette 1 | `#3B9ED8` | primary structure and focal subject |
| Palette 2 | `#77B88C` | supporting contrast and secondary forms |
| Palette 3 | `#F1D87A` | accent for emphasis and directional cues |
| Palette 4 | `#ED7258` | restrained supporting accent |

## Must

- one readable human decision
- one environmental system relationship
- foreground-middle-distance depth
- high-key controlled palette
- concept-first staging
- open destination or threshold

## Never

- travel poster
- scenic sunrise
- flowers or ornamental foreground plants
- tourism landscape
- generic path-to-success
- stock-vector team
- decorative scenery
- photorealistic sky

## Negative constraints

- no text
- no logo
- no UI
- no robot
- no glossy gradient
- no picturesque decoration
- no motivational symbolism
- no travel-advertising finish

## Composition patterns

### Focal metaphor

Best for: core argument, abstract concept.

Build one dominant physical metaphor around the article's central relationship.

### Contrast

Best for: before and after, tradeoff, tension.

Use two clearly different states joined by one meaningful transition.

### Process

Best for: workflow, cause and effect, transformation.

Show only the essential stages and make the change itself visually dominant.

### System relationship

Best for: framework, network, components.

Limit the system to three to five meaningful parts with an obvious hierarchy.

### Narrative scene

Best for: human consequence, decision, editorial tension.

Stage one decisive moment rather than summarizing the whole article.

## Metaphor method

1. Translate the abstract relationship into a physical arrangement.
2. Choose one object family suited to the visual medium.
3. Make the decisive change visible without relying on a headline.

Object pool:

- broad route
- land gate
- open field
- bridge plane
- terraced decision
- river channel
- horizon threshold
- split terrain

Action pool:

- redirect
- cross
- open
- choose
- converge
- leave
- guide
- reconnect

Anti-repetition:

- Change both the main object and the spatial arrangement between illustrations.
- Do not repeat the same composition pattern consecutively.
- Derive metaphors from the current article rather than a stock visual library.

## QA checklist

- environment explains the concept
- human action reads at thumbnail size
- palette stays bright but disciplined
- quiet field remains active
- result feels editorial rather than promotional

Failure signals:

- picturesque but meaningless
- travel-poster mood
- too many plants
- generic journey cliché
- weak human action
- decorative sunset
